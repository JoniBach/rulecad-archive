Bin: optimised lattice wall 0.1.0

2026-10-29 · showcase/bin · Second attempt, at 5% of the cylinder (about 17% of the wall): four legs rise from the foot ring into pointed arches that meet under the rim, like a crown. One piece, nothing in mid-air, no overhang past 45° (the design's ledge under the rim slopes a touch steeper than 45°). 664 g of PLA; leant on 4,240 N/mm, kicked 54 N/mm; 300 steps in an hour on the faster solver, not settled, and 17% of the kept rim had nothing under it before the ledge. On 6 mm cells the optimiser can't bridge (its reach rounds to zero).

Bin: optimised lattice wall 0.1.0

2026-10-29 · showcase/bin · First attempt, too heavy and not yet support-free: 230 × 250 mm, a wall that's mostly solid with tall pointed-arch windows (the 45° rule's own shape), about 2 kg of PLA. The 15% share counted the whole cylinder, the empty inside too, so it filled half the wall; about 4–5% would give a lattice. Leant on: 35,700 N/mm; kicked: 1,380 N/mm. The print check still finds 523 mm² past 45° and two tips under the rim. 246 steps on 76,800 cells of 6 mm in about 4 hours.
