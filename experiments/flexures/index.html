Flexure samplers: twenty-two flexures and mechanisms to measure

30 October 2026 · @rulecad/flexures 0.1.6 · examples/bench/projects/flexures · measurements to come

Four plates, printed flat, with every flexure in @rulecad/flexures bending in the plane of the bed (within its layers), except the LET joint, which folds up out of it. Each has a predicted stiffness and a predicted travel to 2% strain from beam theory and the pseudo-rigid-body model. Measured, they become the material's strain limit and a check on the models. One plate of each in PLA and in PETG, 100% infill, 0.2 mm layers, 0.42 mm lines.

How to measure

Weights at the eyes (coins: a 1p is 3.56 g, a 2p 7.12 g, a £1 8.75 g) or the bench's dial gauge; three readings at increasing loads, the slope is the stiffness. Push each leaf further until it whitens (PLA) or stays bent (PETG): that travel, through the model's strain(travel), is the material's strain limit. The notch and living hinges turn: the angle is the travel at the arm's end over the arm's length. The constant-force mechanism: snap the crank off its tab, pin it, push the slider block along its line and read the force every 2 mm. The LET joint: fold the outer panel up, and note where it whitens first (the bridges, the model says). The third plate: push the folded guide's shuttle toward the rail; push the cartwheel's arm and the elliptical hinge's arm sideways; push each hook's tip clear of its lip and let it click back; pin the ratchet wheel and turn it by the rim. The fourth plate: push the stopped leaf to its post and look for whitening; hang weights on the contact-aided leaf and watch its rate change at 6 mm; push the parallelogram's coupler sideways; squeeze the tweezers shut. Readings go in results.json beside the project, in the fields the file names.

Findings

To come, when the plates are printed and measured. Four questions they answer:

  • What strain does PLA take, and PETG? The rules carry no limit yet on purpose; this is where it comes from.
  • Do the models hold past a few thicknesses of travel? The leaves go further than beam theory's range; the

elastica says how much they stiffen and shorten.

  • Does the LET joint survive in PLA? It is the one flexure that bends across its layers.
  • Is friction 0.3? The snap hooks' and the ratchet's forces assume it; the measured insertion against the deflection force says what it is.
